# IITD Live — Brainstorm #1

Inputs: market research doc, student survey (n=24), sentiment audit (20 sources), web scan of IITD fests (Oct 2026). Event inventory: [`events.csv`](events.csv).

## 1. What's happening at IIT Delhi, and what costs money

12 recurring event families mapped. Fee status:

| Fee status | Count | Events |
|---|---|---|
| Paid entry (confirmed) | 1 | BECon (₹99–₹2,499 tiers; was free for IITD in 2024) |
| Free entry, paid add-ons | 2 | Rendezvous (pronite pass), Tryst (workshops ₹1.2–1.4k) |
| Free / likely free | 5 | Speranza, BRCA inter-hostel, Building India Inc., DMS conclaves, club workshops/talks |
| Unknown | 3 | Sportech, Literati, Parivartan |
| Paid, third-party (trust risk) | 1 | External "workshops at IIT Delhi" (₹1.25–3k) |

**Headline:** almost no IITD event charges a gate fee to IITD students. Money sits in *add-ons* (pronites, workshops, competitions, summit tiers, stalls) and in *outsiders*.

The survey agrees:
- 14/24 attended **zero** paid events last semester; 13/24 spent **₹0**.
- Average spend is about **₹148 per student per semester**.

## 2. Reality check on the market doc's numbers

- The doc models ₹250 per ticket × 8 transactions a year per user, for Year-1 GMV of ₹1.5–2 cr.
- The survey implies ₹148 × 2 semesters × 7,200 users ≈ **₹21 lakh/yr** of internal student GMV.
- At a 5% take rate, that is about **₹1 lakh/yr** of revenue.

→ Student ticket fees alone won't carry the business. The ₹1.5–2 cr only works with outsider passes, workshops, stalls and sponsors added. **Change the pitch to match the data. Don't hide this gap**; showing it is the strongest slide.

## 3. Reframe: free inventory is the hook, B2B is the business

| Layer | Who pays | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Free RSVP + reminders | Nobody | 80% of inventory. Drives daily use and the demand data |
| Outsider passes | External students | RDV / Tryst / BECon draw thousands from DU, other colleges |
| Paid add-ons | Students | Pronite passes, workshops, competition fees, BECon tiers |
| Organiser tools | Societies / fests | QR check-in, analytics, promoted listings |
| Brands & stalls | Sponsors / vendors | Stall booking (₹18–25k at BECon), sponsored events (Pokémon GO × RDV 2026 is a live precedent) |
| Campus commerce | Students | Fest merch, food-stall pre-orders, combo bundles |

## 4. What students actually asked for (Q13, n=24)

1. One feed of upcoming events: **17**
2. Registration + ticketing in one app: **14**
3. Reminders & deadline alerts: **12**
4. QR pass wallet: **9**
5. Live venue/timing updates: **6**
6. Friends attending: **3** · Recommendations: **2**

→ The MVP is feed + register + remind + wallet. Social and recommendation features get little demand; push them out of P1.

Other signals:
- Intent to use is 21/24 at 4–5 out of 5.
- 18/24 missed an event or deadline at least once because they found out late (14 of them twice or more).
- Convenience fee: split evenly between ₹0, ₹1–5, ₹11–20 and "depends on price". Take a % fee on paid tickets and charge nothing on free RSVPs.

## 5. Ideas to debate

- **Verified-organiser badge.** Directly answers the Reddit "is this workshop legit?" / "workshop scam" threads. Cheap to build, high trust.
- **Eligibility labels on every event** (IITD only / open / pass type). The #1 repeated RDV question.
- **IITD-first allocation** for pronites, e.g. an early window for verified students (a survey free-text asked for exactly this).
- **Free-RSVP no-show fix.** 13/24 have skipped an event after registering. Options: waitlist auto-release, or a refundable ₹10 hold.
- **Organiser-side wedge.** Start with one fest (Tryst Feb 2027 or BECon Jan 2027) as the launch partner rather than all societies at once.

## 6. Gaps before the pitch

- Survey is 17/24 MBA and 15/24 first-years. Get **B.Tech responses** (they make up most of the campus and the fest crowd).
- Confirm real prices: RDV pronite pass, Sportech/Parivartan/Literati fees. A WhatsApp to each organiser will do.
- Organiser interviews (target 10): who handles ticketing today (Unstop, Razorpay pages, Google Forms)? Would they pay for check-in and analytics?
